# Decisions log

The suite's decisions, moved out of the root `AGENTS.md` on 2026-11-01 so the charter stays small enough to load whole (field guide T6, WP-8). Read the rows a change touches before making it, and append new rows at the bottom: the date, the decision, the rule it falls under, and who decided it. A row is never edited to change what was decided; a later row supersedes it and says so.

A crate's own decisions stay in its `AGENTS.md` while they are few. A plugin's decisions go in `docs/<plugin>/decisions.md` when it has its own; the suite-wide ones stay here.
